## Direction contract

THESIS: A mechanical engineer's drawing set for software. Each shipped app is an assembly drawing that explodes into its layers in 3D as you scroll. Refuses the dark dev portfolio: name hero, floating phone, bento of project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite drawing sheet ground, vellum linework, safety-orange for leader lines, dimensions, revisions and the primary action; white only for display lettering. Sheet borders with zone letters and numbers, title blocks, balloons, section hatching, revision and BOM tables. Flat linework, no glow, no glass, no gradients. Expanded grotesk caps for lettering, mono only for dimensions and table data.

STORY: The visitor sees a lead mobile engineer who ships production apps (five live on Google Play, measured results), with full-stack and AI range, a playful person behind it, and takes the CV or emails.
