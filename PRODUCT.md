## Product Principles
1. Proof before adjectives: every claim carries a number, a shipped app, or a named employer.
2. One click to the CV, from anywhere on the page.
3. The craft of the site is itself evidence: motion must feel engineered, smooth, and never block reading.
4. Personality is a feature, not filler: keep it short and real.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
